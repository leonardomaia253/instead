# Initial LinkedIn Campaign — Instead

## Strategic direction

Goal: start qualified conversations with founders, Web3 communities, agencies and businesses that need to structure or launch a digital asset without carrying the entire technical burden alone.

Entry offer: a short tokenomics and permissions review, followed by an assisted deployment when there is a fit. This creates a low-friction first engagement and demonstrates Instead’s central value: simplicity backed by review and responsible execution.

Voice: direct, confident and educational. Avoid return claims, unnecessary jargon and language that positions the product as a speculative shortcut.

Suggested cadence: publish on Tuesdays and Thursdays for two weeks, followed by the fifth post on the next Tuesday. Reply to comments on the same day and only message people who show genuine intent.

## Post — Your token should not be born in the dark

Creative: `creatives-final-v2/your-token-dark.png`

Creating a token has become easier.

Making the right decisions before deployment has not.

Supply, permissions, fees, minting, burning and administrative controls can completely change a project’s risk profile. When those choices are treated as minor details, the problem only becomes visible after the contract is already live.

Instead reverses that logic: clarity first, execution second.

The guided flow helps configure the asset. The pre-deployment review helps teams understand what each choice actually means. Multichain execution gives the project a more structured path from idea to network.

If you are building a digital asset and want a second set of eyes before launch, comment “review” or send us a message.

## Post — Assisted deployment is only the beginning

Creative: `creatives-final-v2/assisted-deployment.png`

A contract being onchain does not mean the project is ready.

A credible launch starts before deployment and continues after it:

- reviewed parameters;
- clearly understood permissions;
- a network chosen for the right reasons;
- documented next steps;
- risks communicated without spin.

That is why Instead does not treat assisted deployment as a simple button. It is the first step in building trust between technology, operations and the community.

We are opening conversations with early projects and agencies that need to launch digital assets for their clients with more structure and less improvisation.

If that sounds like your situation, send us “deploy.” The first conversation is a no-obligation diagnostic.

## Post — The decision comes before the contract

Creative: `creatives-final-v2/decision-before-contract.png`

The most expensive mistake in a launch can happen before the first line of the contract is written.

It happens when nobody asks:

- why this token needs to exist;
- who actually needs each permission;
- how supply will be distributed;
- which controls could undermine trust;
- what the community should understand from day one.

Technology executes decisions. It cannot fix a poorly structured thesis.

That is why Instead combines a guided creation flow with parameter analysis and a pre-deployment review. The goal is not simply to put more tokens onchain. It is to help strong projects start with greater clarity.

We are selecting our first projects for a tokenomics and permissions diagnostic. Send us a short project overview if you would like yours assessed.

#Tokenomics #Blockchain #Governance

## Post — Liquidity requires risk visibility

Creative: `creatives-final-v2/liquidity-risk.png`

In DeFi, speed without context can be expensive.

Before taking collateralized credit, users need to understand collateral, debt, health factor and the impact of a market move. A transaction may take seconds. The decision should not.

Instead’s vision for lending is simple: make risk visible before signature, keep the position under the user’s control and translate technical metrics into practical decisions.

This module is still progressing carefully through configuration and validation for each network. We do not sell urgency as innovation.

If you use lending regularly, what would you like to understand more clearly before opening a position?

## Response qualification

Prioritize contacts that already have a project, audience, client or defined use case. The first conversation should establish the asset’s purpose, preferred network, tokenomics stage, timeline and decision-maker. Never promise an audit, financial returns or absolute security.
